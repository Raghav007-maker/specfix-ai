# NOTIF-56 Weekly digest email

## Description

Users have asked for a summary instead of getting pinged on every single event. We
should send a weekly digest that rolls up what happened on their projects.

Marketing wants this to look good and drive people back into the product. Send it
early in the week so it lands before people get busy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- User receives a digest email each week
- Digest lists activity from their projects
- User can turn the digest off
- Email renders correctly on mobile
